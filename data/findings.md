# Key Findings

## Transaction Success

The majority of transactions were successful, indicating a stable digital payment ecosystem.

## Payment Modes

QR-based payments represented a significant share of total transactions.

## Merchant Categories

Groceries, Fuel, Food & Dining, and Online Services generated substantial transaction activity.

## Location Analysis

Urban and Semi-Urban regions showed higher transaction participation compared to Rural locations.

## Recommendations

- Improve payment reliability in low-performing segments.
- Promote high-adoption payment channels.
- Target merchant categories with strong transaction volumes.
- Develop region-specific customer engagement strategies.
